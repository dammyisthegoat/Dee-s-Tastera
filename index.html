How It Works

Ordering, simply.

  1. 1

    Choose your meals

    Browse the menu and add what you’d like. You can change quantities any time before you send.

  2. 2

    Send it on WhatsApp

    Add your name, phone number and how you’d like to get your food. Your order opens as a ready-written WhatsApp message to Dee.

  3. 3

    Confirm with Dee

    Dee replies to confirm your order and total, and to sort out collection or delivery with you.
